# Nutrition catalog

The bundled catalog is a reviewed MVP fixture. Nutrition values are normalized per 100g/ml and package prices are illustrative US estimates. The shared engine owns all calculations; AI may rank or explain catalog recipes but cannot invent nutrition or prices.

Before production launch, replace each row with a provenance URL, effective date, region/currency, unit conversions, and a review owner. Do not present estimated nutrition or grocery prices as medical advice or a checkout quote.
